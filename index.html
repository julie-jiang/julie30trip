The Destination: Sayulita, Mexico

Sayulita is a tiny, charming boho surf town on Mexico's Pacific coast. Ian and I went last year and it's the perfect blend of everything — great weather, very walkable town, lots of fun activities. The entire town is 4 blocks by 6 blocks — you can walk everywhere, great for late night bar hopping.

🏄

Things We Can Do

Surfing

One of the best beginner surf spots in Mexico. Lessons are cheap, break is sandy and mellow. Jan–Mar is ideal season.
